    Yoga

    FEATURED

    Warrior I

    A beginner level pose, Warrior Pose I is one of the three Warrior Poses that beautifully combines both the standing and balancing action of the body.

    Bridge

    Strengthen your posterior chain — or the backside of your body — with a Bridge.

    Bow

    Stretch the entire front side of your body while strengthening your back in this pose.

    King Pigeon

    Open your hips and stretch your abdominals with King Pigeon, a progression of Pigeon Pose.

    Dove

    Stretch your back and abs — plus strengthen your shoulders and legs — with Dove Pose.

    Lord of the Dance

    Improve your balance and flexibility and stretch the whole front side of your body with Lord of the Dance.

    Headstand

    Build upper body and core strength, as well as improve your balance and even circulation, with a Headstand.

    Boat

    It activates the abdominal muscles, hip flexors, and spine, making it an effective pose for cultivating inner strength and focus.

    Garland

    Garland Pose opens the hips and groins as it stretches and strengthens the feet and ankles, Open your hips, thighs, and ankles with Garland Pose.

    Peacock

    Work on your arm strength and balance with Peacock Pose, Another way to prepare to do the pose with legs lifted is to raise one foot at a time. Practice gradually shifting more weight away from your feet and onto your arms.
