    KEY TERMS

    Keto meal plan

    A ketogenic, or "keto," meal plan outlines a way of eating that is very low in carbs and high in fat. You consume about 75% of calories from fat, 5% from carbs, and 20% from protein.

    This can promote weight loss, but it's considered highly restrictive and not suitable for everyone.

    Learn More:

    Mediterranean diet meal plan

    The Mediterranean diet emphasizes vegetables, fruits, fish, whole grains, and healthy fats like olive oil. It’s well-studied and supports heart health.

    Meals often include grilled fish, salads, legumes, and nuts.

    Learn More:

    Whole 30 meal plan

    The Whole 30 plan removes sugar, alcohol, grains, legumes, soy, and dairy for 30 days. It helps identify food sensitivities and encourages whole foods.

    After 30 days, foods are slowly reintroduced to test their effects on your body.
